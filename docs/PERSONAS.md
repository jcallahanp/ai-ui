# Personas

**Status:** not started. Written after the requirements are agreed.

A persona is a real kind of person from the interview, not an invented one. Each has "done
when" checks: things that person must be able to do, written so that someone looking at a
screen can answer yes or no. The design is finished when every check is a yes.

## Persona format

```
## P1: <name or role>
- Who they are: <one or two sentences>
- What they are trying to get done: <their goal, in their words where possible>
- Where and how they use it: <device, setting, how often>
- What gets in their way today: <from the interview>
- Done when:
  - P1.1: <a yes-or-no check against a screen>
  - P1.2: <another>
```
